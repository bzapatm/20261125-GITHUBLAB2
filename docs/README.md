# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## About

OctoAcme's project management approach is customer-first and iterative. Projects move through initiation, planning, execution, release, and close/retrospective phases, with clear ownership and regular communication throughout.

## Project Management Process Overview

- **Initiation:** Define the problem, success measures, stakeholders, scope, and initial timeline.
- **Planning:** Establish scope, resources, milestones, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and delivery plans.
- **Execution:** Deliver work in small increments, use project boards and test automation, perform reviews, track quality, and manage risks and blockers.
- **Release:** Deploy, verify, and communicate the result using a defined release and rollback approach.
- **Close and retrospective:** Capture outcomes, lessons learned, and actionable improvements for future work.

The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications. The Product Manager owns the product outcomes, backlog priorities, and success metrics. Developers and QA collaborate to implement and validate work against acceptance criteria. Stakeholders provide direction and receive regular updates.

Teams maintain visibility through weekly PM/PdM alignment, twice-weekly delivery standups or an agreed equivalent, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalation when needed.
